# Chess Professor Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 8, 2026_

Chess Professor is built to work entirely on your device. We do not run servers for the app, we do not have user accounts, and we do not collect, sell, or share your personal information.

## What stays on your device

The following is stored only on your iPhone or iPad, using Apple's standard on-device storage:

- Your games, moves, and coach reviews
- Player profiles you create (names and avatars you choose)
- Puzzle results, lesson progress, and progress statistics
- Your settings, such as board theme, sounds, and haptics

This information is never sent to us or to any third party. If you delete the app, it is removed from your device. It may be included in your own device or iCloud backups, which Apple manages under your Apple account.

## What we do not collect

- No analytics, advertising, or tracking tools
- No advertising identifiers and no cross-app tracking
- No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access
- No accounts, email addresses, or passwords

## Purchases

Chess Professor Pro is available as an annual subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Apple that you own Pro, so it can unlock features. Subscriptions are managed in your Apple account settings, and you can use "Restore Purchases" in the app to restore a previous purchase on a new device.

## Notifications

If you turn on the optional weekly summary, the app schedules a local notification on your device. It is created on your device from your own data, and nothing is sent to a server. You can turn it off at any time in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings.

## Children

Chess Professor does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. There are no ads, no chat, and no external links apart from the legal pages and Apple's subscription management.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
