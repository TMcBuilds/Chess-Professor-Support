# Chess Professor Support

Chess Professor is a chess coach for iPhone and iPad. It teaches as you play, with hints, plain-English move explanations, lessons, puzzles and progress tracking. Everything runs on your device.

**Need help?** Email [tmac35@gmail.com](mailto:tmac35@gmail.com). Please include your device model, your iOS version and a short description of what happened. Screenshots help. I aim to reply within two business days.

## Free and Pro

**Free** includes unlimited games against the computer at the Beginner and Casual levels, 10 hints per game, basic coaching, the core lessons, 5 puzzles a day, one player profile, your last 10 games, simple progress stats and one full game review a week.

**Pro** adds every level, full coach reviews of your games, training from your own mistakes, unlimited puzzles and the full lesson library, full progress insights and the weekly summary, unlimited history with PGN export, extra player profiles, the 3D board and extra themes.

Pro is an annual subscription (with a free trial for new subscribers) or a one-time lifetime purchase.

## Purchases and subscriptions

**Restore a purchase.** Open the app, tap the gear icon for Settings and choose Restore Purchases. Sign in to the same Apple Account you used to buy.

**Cancel or change a subscription.** On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Chess Professor. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

**Refunds.** Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**Lifetime purchase.** It is tied to your Apple Account and can be restored on any of your devices with Restore Purchases.

## Common questions

**Why can't I pick a harder level?** Intermediate, Advanced and Challenger are part of Pro.

**My puzzles ran out.** Free play includes 5 puzzles a day. The count resets each day. Pro has no limit.

**I can't see my older games.** Free keeps your last 10 games in History. Your older games are not deleted, and they return if you upgrade to Pro.

**How do I use the coach?** Tap the graduation cap during a game to hear what just happened and what to look for next. Tap Hint to highlight a good move. After a game, open the review to see where it turned.

**What do the move notations mean (like Nf3)?** Tap the legend button in the move list for a short guide to chess notation.

**Can I play a friend?** Yes. Choose A friend on the New Game screen for pass-and-play on one device.

**Can I make my own profile?** Yes. Tap your name at the top left of the Play screen to add or switch players. Each player keeps their own games and progress. Free includes one profile and Pro adds more.

**The weekly summary notification isn't showing.** Turn it on in the app's Settings, and make sure notifications are allowed for Chess Professor in the iOS Settings app.

**Does the app need an internet connection?** No. It works offline. Only buying or restoring Pro needs a connection.

## Your data

Your games, profiles and progress are stored only on your device, and I never receive them. Deleting the app removes them, so back up your device if you want to keep them. Read the full [Privacy Policy](privacy).
